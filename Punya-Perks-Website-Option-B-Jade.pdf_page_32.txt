G L O S S A R Y
Loyalty terms, defined
plainly.
The vocabulary used across the product, the docs and our
guides.
Suggest a term
Breakage
Points that expire or are never redeemed; a liability release for finance.
Earn
Any event that credits points to a member.
Burn
Redemption of points for a reward.
Escheatment
Unclaimed-property rules that may apply to cash-convertible balances.
Lot
A batch of points with a common expiry date.
Maker-checker
A control where the person who initiates a change cannot approve it.
Pending points
Earned points held until a return window passes.
Tier
A status level with its own multipliers and perks.
Quote-then-commit
A checkout pattern that holds points for a timed quote before confirming.
DSR
Data-subject request: access, correction, erasure or opt-out.
