[P] Calendar embed to pick a slot; confirmation screen with what to expect; email confirmation.

[P] Verify email (code screen).

[P] Design brief and open decisions
[P] Brief for designers
[P] Tone: confident, precise, enterprise-credible; short headlines (max 8 words), one idea per section.
[P] Visual system: Crisp (white canvas, grey surfaces, ink pills, gold accent, Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter); product UI shown in real screenshots with light device frames, never abstract illustration alone.
[P] Every page has one primary CTA; demo for buyers, sandbox for developers.
[P] Accessibility WCAG 2.2 AA; RTL layout for Arabic; locale-aware numbers and currency on Pricing.
[P] Performance budget: hero visible under 2.5 s on 4G; no autoplay video on mobile.
[P] Deliver desktop 1440, tablet 834, mobile 390 for each template; component library in the same Figma file as the app designs.
