Who this applies to
Any programme enrolling Indian residents, whether the operator is in India or abroad.
Obligations scale with what the points can become.
Consent under DPDP
Consent must be specific, informed and withdrawable. Capture it per purpose with a
version and timestamp, and re-capture on material change. [Expand with counsel-
reviewed text.]
SMS and WhatsApp templates
Promotional SMS requires DLT entity, header and template registration; WhatsApp
templates are categorised as marketing, utility or authentication. Quiet hours apply to
promotional traffic.
When points become money
Cash-convertible points bring payout-partner, KYC and escheatment considerations.
Keep cash redemption off by default and gate it per tenant.
Record keeping
Ledger entries, consent versions and KYC status references should be retained per the
schedule your DPO sets; the platform minimums cannot be relaxed by a tenant.
Checklist
Consent purposes defined and versioned
DLT entity and templates registered
WhatsApp templates categorised
Cash redemption gated and partner contracted
Retention schedule approved by DPO
DSR workflow tested end to end
