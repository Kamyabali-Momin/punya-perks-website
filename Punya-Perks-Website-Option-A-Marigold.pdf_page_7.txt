H O W  I T  F I T S  T O G E T H E R
Transactions in, loyalty out.

Plugin, signed webhook or
scheduled batch.
Idempotent, timezone-
aware, line-item aware.
02 Decide
Rules evaluate eligibility,
multipliers, caps and
campaigns, versioned at
transaction time.

Append-only hash-

A D M I N I S T R A T I O N
Two consoles, one truth.
Super Admin runs the platform: tenants, plans, connectors, billing,
security, data privacy centre. Tenant Admin runs the programme: rules,
campaigns, catalog, tiers, theming, consent, fraud. Every change is
maker-checker where money moves.
